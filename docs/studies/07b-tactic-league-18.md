# Study 7b: an 18-tactic league, and which matchups stand out

**Status:** done (12 November, 6,120 matches). A second league on neutral ground, run from the app's League page,
with newer versions of several community tactics and one more; it also asks whether particular matchups matter.

## Design

- **18 tactics:** the 10 installed FM27 presets and 8 community tactics: ZaZ's Ace of Clubs, Diamonds, Hearts and
  Spades (version 1.0; Study 7 had 0.1), STORMBRINGER 27 BBMIW 424 P102, ARGUS 27v3 3421 P103, HIGHWAY STAR 27 4231
  P101 and TIME 27 4321 P100.
- **The same squad for every team:** the synthetic Premier League middle-eight reference squad. The two template
  matches have 20 squad slots per side, so every team used the best 20 of its 23 players.
- **No real team involved:** fixtures alternate between two template matches between other clubs (Portsmouth v
  Crystal Palace, Everton v Fulham); only their stadium and conditions are used.
- **Equal conditions:** both sides start alike, tactical familiarity is 70 in all eight areas for every team, each side
  picks its XI by position, and the assistant manages both sides.
- **20 seasons**, home and away: 34 matches per tactic per season, 680 in all.

## Do particular matchups matter?

Each pairing was compared with what the two tactics' overall strengths predict (expected points of A against B =
average + strength of A − strength of B, fitted to all 306 pairings).

- **Only one pairing differs clearly** (by three standard errors or more): Control Possession takes 0.88 expected
  points per match against Ace of Clubs, against 1.12 predicted (−0.24).
- **Twelve differ by two to three standard errors, about as many as chance alone would produce (14).**

So in this league **a tactic's overall strength predicts almost all its head-to-head results:** there is no sign of
"rock, paper, scissors" between styles, such as a strong tactic that falls apart against a low block. Study 7's league
showed a little more matchup variation (27 pairings at two to three standard errors, against 12 expected by chance,
and four beyond three), so matchup effects exist, but they are small next to the differences in overall strength.

## Against Study 7

The same design as Study 7 (neutral ground, synthetic squads, 20 seasons) with other template matches, familiarity
70 instead of 80, and newer versions of several tactics.

- **The presets reproduce:** each is within about 0.06 points per game of its Study 7 value (Gegenpress 1.37 / 1.39,
  Fluid Counter-Attack 1.37 / 1.38, Park the Bus 0.90 / 0.92), and every community tactic again beats every preset.
- **The newer Aces are stronger:** Diamonds 1.0 1.67 (0.1: 1.59), Hearts 1.0 1.57 (0.1: 1.44); Clubs is level (1.70)
  and Spades about level (1.52 / 1.50).
- STORMBRINGER, the same tactic, is 1.71 here and 1.78 in Study 7: within the two leagues' combined uncertainty.

## Caveats

- **One squad level** (Premier League middle eight) and **20-slot matchday squads** from these templates.
- **Equal, fixed familiarity (70).** In a real save a new tactic starts lower: see Study 3.
- **Matchups are averaged over both venues and 20 seasons;** a matchup effect smaller than about 0.2 expected points
  per match would not show.

## Reproduce

In the app: **Test a league**, add the presets and the tactic files, squad *Synthetic: Premier League, middle eight*,
familiarity 70, 20 seasons, two template matches between other clubs. Then:

```sh
.venv/bin/python port/validation/league_matchups.py data/leagues/<league id>     # table, xPts head to head, matchups
```
